# Asset provenance

## Artwork

`Apple/App/GalleonMaster.png` is original generated artwork created for this
project with the built-in image-generation tool on 11 September 2026. No input
franchise artwork or logos were supplied. The design is a gold coin, original
compass star and open-book emblem on a dark emerald field. No text or characters.

The prompt requested a high-contrast square icon suitable for iPhone and circular
Watch masking, with gold/ivory/emerald colouring, no rounded outer corners, no
franchise insignia, no actor likeness, no trademark symbols and no lettering.
`Scripts/generate_assets.py` exports the required 1024px asset-catalog variants.

`Apple/App/QuizChamber.png` is new original scenery generated with the built-in
image tool for the automatic-play update. It replaces the in-game circular
emblem, not the app icon. The unchanged bitmap is copied into both QuizChamber
image sets. It is decorative and is excluded from VoiceOver navigation.

Generation prompt: Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production bitmap
scenery for Galleonaire, an original magical knowledge quiz on iPhone and Apple
Watch. Create one polished portrait 4:5 composition of an enchanted quiz chamber,
with a magnificent open book levitating just above a finely made angular brass
lectern in a verdant green vaulted library. The book is the central, clearly
readable visual subject: individually sculpted cream pages, emerald binding,
warm gold light travelling along a few elegant angular etched lines from the
book into the chamber, suggesting knowledge awakening. Architecture frames the
book with crisp geometric gold filigree, rich emerald glass and small deep
cranberry accents, professional premium game art with tactile materials,
painterly 3D realism, not cartoon clip art. Keep the upper quarter quiet and dark
enough for native interface text to be overlaid, and retain spacious calm margins
for mobile cropping. The book and lectern must remain sharply visible at small
watch sizes. Restrained lighting, no overexposed glow. Deliver scene only,
absolutely no letters, numbers, text, UI controls or frame. No coins, circular
emblems, magic circles, discrete glowing orbs, bokeh dots, particle confetti,
wands, characters, animals, Hogwarts castle, house crests, lightning symbols or
borrowed franchise imagery. This is original Galleonaire scenery, not an app icon.

## Audio

All fifteen WAV files are original procedural compositions generated by
`Scripts/generate_assets.py`, created for Sidney Tambin's Galleonaire project.
There are no third-party recordings, samples, loops or imported melodies, and no
third-party sample licence or attribution obligation. This is not a claim to
licence the Harry Potter franchise or its music.

- `magical-library`: original 24-second six-bar celesta/pad composition.
- `selected`: single high chime.
- `locked`: low double strike with filtered air.
- `correct`: bright upper-register ascending major-bell flourish and resolved chord.
- `incorrect`: dramatic 2.8-second descending low-brass phrase, deep impact and bass tail.
- `fiftyFifty`: two short falling pops with airy transients.
- `audience`: original synthesized applause, 28 randomized hand-clap-like noise transients.
- `swapQuestion`: filtered page-turn-like air with a rising shimmer.
- `lifelineSelected`: two close bright accents.
- `lifelineActivated`: ascending spell-like sweep with filtered noise.
- `lifelineResult`: resolved two-note answer cue.
- `nextQuestion`: low-to-high transition with soft air.
- `milestone`: sustained chord and two high accents.
- `majorMilestone`: low sustained fifth and rising upper phrase.
- `victory`: original 6.6-second celebratory major-key fanfare, brass-like lead,
  layered bells, timpani-like impacts, reverberant final chord and synthesized applause.

The older selected/locked/generic lifeline files remain in the media collection
but are not used by the new immediate-answer flow. Every active effect has its
own Settings preview button. Music defaults to 15 percent, effects to 35 percent.
Both controls now use the full 0-100 percent range, including with VoiceOver.
No speech synthesis or spoken question audio is used.
Real-device sound balance, interruptions and Watch output require listening
tests; generation alone does not verify audibility or speech compatibility.
