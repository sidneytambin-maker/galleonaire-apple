# Public 1.0 content and accessibility review — 25 November 2026

196 additions extend the existing 1,304-question collection to 1,500. All new items enter Classic and the relevant exam pool. IDs, old Classic levels, historical records and saved paper snapshots are preserved. The seven original novels, companion-book creature and Quidditch lore, official Rowling essays, the official encyclopedia and film credits supply the new facts. Each addition carries its source and version. Source checks and editorial review cover the question, four choices, keyed answer, explanation and assessed difficulty. Difficulty has not been calibrated against player data.

Drafts repeating Mrs Cole's role, Fabian's watch, Regulus's middle name, Dobby's wages, St Mungo's shop frontage and Dissendium were replaced. Additional wording was changed to avoid revealing the identity of Grindelwald's prison or the Black brothers' relationship. Matched film titles, coincidental numerical answers and distinct character/actor pairings were inspected individually. Similarity decisions are recorded in Reference/exam-similarity-review.json. Distinct facts within a shared topic are retained; a lexical scan cannot prove that no human will regard any two questions as related.

The shared Exam Next Question hint now says “Moves to the next question”. The button retains its label for answered and unanswered items. Existing focus requests target the new heading. The briefing describes a continuing timer only when a timed N.E.W.T. option is selected. Close controls say “Close” outside an active saved paper. The Classic answer hint no longer promises a next question after the final answer. About shows the actual app version and distinguishes resumable exams from unfinished Classic games. Other shared hints were checked against their action, including the five-percent VoiceOver volume adjustment and sound-preview volume.

Public What's New introduces the 1,500-question collection, both Exam Modes and clearer Statistics. It deliberately does not describe the beta-only VoiceOver bug as a public regression. Statistics' existing redesign separates Classic records, topic performance and exam results, with one accessible summary per statistic and detail navigation.

Validation uses the existing deterministic bank validator, Swift core suite and three focused UI scenarios per platform on the established cloud Xcode runner. The scenarios cover O.W.L. answers, next/previous, submission and review; N.E.W.T. next, skip and resume; and Classic/exam/results/legacy-statistics smoke states. Actual results and runtime versions will be recorded in the release evidence. Code inspection and simulator checks do not establish physical-device VoiceOver speech timing.

The request is for public App Store review, with automatic release after approval. The distribution route branches on the explicit app-store channel and does not assign this build to TestFlight groups or submit Beta App Review. Submission completion requires an exact version/build and review-item read-back from Apple, not an upload receipt.

Final outcome: version 1.0.0 build 52 passed 98 Python checks, 63 Swift core tests and three UI scenarios per platform on Xcode 27.2, iOS 27.2 and watchOS 27.2. Two stale test expectations were corrected; the successful iPhone job was reused and the Watch focused suite passed after correction. The signed package contains the same reviewed 1,500-question bank on both platforms.
